CampMonitor Alpha 7.1 display behaviour

- Default brightness: 75%.
- Hold the screen continuously for 2 seconds to increase brightness.
- Brightness levels: 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, then back to 25%.
- After 10 minutes without touch, the TFT backlight switches off.
- Touch once to wake the screen. That first touch only wakes it and will not activate a page button.
- BLE monitoring and all background processing continue while the backlight is off.
- Brightness is intentionally not stored in NVS in this build.
